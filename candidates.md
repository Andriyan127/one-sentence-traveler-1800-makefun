# Candidates & scoring — "One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800"

Scored 0–25 (5 criteria × 5 points): brief fit / no post-1800 words /
vividness & specificity / tightness / gate compliance (1–200 chars).
Every word of every candidate was checked against the 1800-vocabulary
rule (banned: internet, computer, Wi-Fi, screen, phone, website, email,
digital, online, app, data, network, video, telegraph, photograph, etc.).

## Round 1 — four genuinely different angles

### A1 — the sleepless market (140 chars) — 22/25
> Every house now keeps a small window onto a market that never closes, where strangers trade news, songs, and stories at the speed of gossip.
- Brief fit 4 · vocab 5 · vividness 4 · tightness 4 · gate 5
- Strong picture, but "trade news, songs, and stories" drifts toward a feature list.

### B1 — the answering library (129 chars) — 24/25 ★ leader
> Imagine a library where every book answers back, and any letter you write reaches its reader in the time it takes a bell to ring.
- Brief fit 5 · vocab 5 · vividness 5 · tightness 4 · gate 5
- One picture (library + letter), two internet properties at once: interactivity and instant delivery.

### C1 — the mirror in every home (126 chars) — 21/25
> It is a mirror hung in every home that shows, at any hour, what any town crier, singer, or scholar in the world wishes to say.
- Brief fit 4 · vocab 5 · vividness 4 · tightness 4 · gate 5
- Vivid, but leans on listing roles (crier, singer, scholar) and says little about instant exchange.

### D1 — the tavern that never empties (146 chars) — 21/25
> Picture a tavern that never empties, where every table holds a stranger eager to swap a story, a song, or the news from a town you will never see.
- Brief fit 4 · vocab 5 · vividness 4 · tightness 3 · gate 5
- Warm and social, but wordy and again a small list (story, song, news).

## Improvement pass 1 — tighten the leader (B1 → B2)

### B2 (120 chars) — 24/25
> Imagine a library where every book answers back, and every letter finds its reader before the bell has finished ringing.
- Brief fit 5 · vocab 5 · vividness 5 · tightness 4 · gate 5
- "every letter" (not "any letter you write") is tighter; "has finished ringing" is still wordy.

## Improvement pass 2 — sharpen the image (B2 → B3)

### B3 (120 chars) — 25/25 ★ WINNER
> Imagine a library where every book answers back, and every letter reaches its reader before a struck bell stops ringing.
- Brief fit 5 · vocab 5 · vividness 5 · tightness 5 · gate 5
- "a struck bell stops ringing" is a concrete, audible 1800 image for
  instant delivery — tighter and more sensory than B2, same length.
- Word check: imagine, library, every, book, answers, back, and, letter,
  reaches, its, reader, before, struck, bell, stops, ringing — all
  plausibly in use in 1800. No post-1800 word anywhere.
